简要说明：秀鹿英文阅读器是一款离线优先的阅读应用，我们高度重视您的隐私保护，仅收集必要的数据以提供服务。

📱 应用概述

秀鹿英文阅读器是一款专注于英文电子书阅读的移动应用，支持PDF、EPUB、TXT等多种格式，提供翻译、词典查询、语音朗读等功能。

2.1 我们收集的信息

  • 网络数据：用于翻译服务和在线词典查询
  • 音频设置：用于文字转语音(TTS)朗读功能
  • 阅读记录：您的阅读进度、书签、笔记等，仅存储在本地设备

2.2 数据用途

  • 提供电子书阅读服务
  • 保存您的阅读进度和偏好设置
  • 提供翻译和词典查询服务
  • 播放语音朗读功能
  • 改善应用性能和用户体验

🛡️ 权限说明

  • 存储权限：读取您设备上的电子书文件
  • 网络权限：访问翻译API和在线词典
  • 音频权限：调整音频设置以支持TTS朗读
  • 震动权限：提供触觉反馈增强用户体验
重要说明：我们不会录制您的语音或获取麦克风权限，所有音频功能仅用于播放。

💾 数据存储与安全

  • 您的阅读数据主要存储在本地设备，确保隐私安全
  • 网络传输采用HTTPS加密协议
  • 我们不会将您的个人数据出售给第三方
  • 仅在必要时与可信的服务提供商共享数据（如翻译API）

🔒 Xiulu English Reader - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 26, 2024
Overview: Xiulu English Reader is an offline-first reading app that highly values your privacy protection and only collects necessary data to provide services.

📱 App Overview

Xiulu English Reader is a mobile application focused on English e-book reading, supporting multiple formats including PDF, EPUB, TXT, and providing translation, dictionary lookup, and text-to-speech features.

🔍 Data Collection & Usage

2.1 Information We Collect

  • File Access: To enable e-book reading, we need access to document files on your device
  • Network Data: Used for translation services and online dictionary queries
  • Audio Settings: Used for text-to-speech (TTS) functionality
  • Reading Records: Your reading progress, bookmarks, notes, stored locally on your device only

2.2 Data Usage

  • Provide e-book reading services
  • Save your reading progress and preference settings
  • Provide translation and dictionary services
  • Enable text-to-speech functionality
  • Improve app performance and user experience

🛡️ Permissions Explained

  • Storage Permission: Read e-book files on your device
  • Network Permission: Access translation APIs and online dictionaries
  • Audio Permission: Adjust audio settings to support TTS
  • Vibration Permission: Provide haptic feedback for enhanced user experience
Important Note: We do not record your voice or request microphone permissions. All audio features are for playback only.

💾 Data Storage & Security

  • Your reading data is primarily stored locally on your device for privacy security
  • Network transmissions use HTTPS encryption protocol
  • We will not sell your personal data to third parties
  • Data is shared with trusted service providers only when necessary (such as translation APIs)

🔄 Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Translation Services: For real-time translation functionality
  • Online Dictionaries: Provide more accurate word definitions
  • Voice Services: Provide online pronunciation features

These services have their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing their terms.

👤 User Rights

  • You can delete the app and all its local data at any time
  • You can disable specific features (such as online translation) in settings
  • You can contact us to request data deletion
  • You have the right to understand how we use your data

📞 Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this privacy policy, please contact us through:

  • Email:xiuluapp@gmail.com
  • In-App Feedback: Submit feedback through the app settings page

We will respond within 48 hours of receiving your message.

📋 Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Major changes will be communicated to users through in-app notifications. We recommend reviewing this policy regularly for the latest information.
